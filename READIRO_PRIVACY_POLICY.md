## Privacy policy

Readiro is a learning app with short lessons and a previous library of optional interactive practice stories. This policy explains what the app stores, what it sends to other services, and how to contact us. Readiro does not require an account or a date of birth.

## Information stored on your device

Readiro stores the IDs of lessons you mark as read or bookmark and your current unfinished lesson. It also stores your selected practice age range and learning interests, onboarding status, story choices and progress, completed stories, and any lesson takeaways you choose to save. A saved takeaway is curated story content, not a personal journal entry. You can also mark a suggested activity as tried. The app may keep local access to a story that was available to you before an access-policy change. A small local setting helps limit how often the app asks eligible adult-path learners for an App Store rating.

This information is used to suggest unread lessons, resume lessons and stories, show progress, and remember saved ideas. Readiro does not collect answers to reflection prompts. Learning information remains in the app's local storage and is not sent to a developer-operated server. The app has no account system, advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs, public profiles, community messaging, or AI chat.

## Purchases

If you choose the one-time Lifetime Unlock, Apple's StoreKit handles the purchase and restoration. Readiro checks Apple's verified entitlement information to unlock eligible content. We do not receive your payment-card details through the app. Apple handles transaction and Apple Account information under its own policies. Loading a price, purchasing, or restoring may require a network connection.

## Backups, retention, and deletion

Learning information is retained on your device until you remove it or delete the app. You can remove a lesson bookmark using its bookmark button and remove saved practice takeaways in Saved. Deleting Readiro removes its local learning data from that device; a device backup may still contain a copy until you manage or delete the backup through your device or backup provider. Readiro does not provide developer-hosted progress sync. Restoring a purchase restores eligible access, not deleted learning progress. Apple manages purchase records separately.

## Children and families

Readiro’s optional practice library offers paths for ages 8–12 and 13–17. Reading lessons does not require age selection. The optional practice library asks for an age range rather than a birthday and does not ask children to create an account or public profile. Optional purchase information for younger paths and readers who have not selected an age is directed to a parent or guardian. A parent or guardian should handle purchases and support requests for a younger learner. The app's adult check is a prompt, not identity verification.

## Support and external policy page

If you email us for support, we receive the address and message you choose to send. We use that information to respond to your request. Please avoid sending a child's personal information, private reflections, payment details, or other sensitive information. You can contact us to ask about or request deletion of your support correspondence; we will handle requests under applicable requirements.

This policy is hosted on GitHub. If you open the public policy page from Readiro or an App Store listing, GitHub may process information about your visit under [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). Opening the page is optional; it is separate from using Readiro's learning features.

## App support

### Contact

[gsaikrishna28@gmail.com](mailto:gsaikrishna28@gmail.com)

Include your app version (Settings → About Readiro & help), device model, iOS version, and a short explanation of what happened. Avoid sending payment details, a child's personal information or private reflections. If attaching screenshots, remove personal information first. Parents or guardians should contact support on behalf of younger learners.

### Restore lifetime access

Open Settings → Lifetime Unlock & Restore → Restore Purchases. Use the Apple Account associated with the eligible purchase and an internet connection. Younger learners will see an adult-directed gate first. A pending purchase may still need approval. Apple manages refunds and payment disputes.

### Lessons and saved ideas

Learn offers your next unread lesson. Tap Finish lesson to mark it read, and the bookmark icon to save or remove it independently. All lessons lets you read in any order or revisit a lesson. The first three lessons are free; the remaining thirteen require the one-time Lifetime Unlock. There is no daily reset or subscription. An unfinished lesson is offered again after relaunch, from its beginning rather than its previous scroll position.

Saved holds bookmarked lessons and any takeaways saved from the original practice stories. Your progress stays on this device. Deleting the app removes local progress; purchase restoration does not restore it.

### Previous practice library

Open Learn → Settings → Previous practice library → Open previous library, choose the practice age range and tap Browse practice stories. The history icon opens previous practice progress. Replaying explores alternate decisions without increasing completed-story counts. Each age path has three free introductions; premium inventory and existing lifetime purchases are unchanged. The 8–12, 13–17 and 25+ paths include eight premium stories; the 18–24 path includes thirteen. Existing learners may retain previously started Life/Career introductions under the earlier access policy.

### Offline use

Bundled lessons and stories work offline. Price loading, purchasing, and restoration can require a network connection. Access depends on the entitlement information available through StoreKit; contact support if eligible access does not appear after restoration.

### Accessibility

Readiro follows device text size and appearance settings. Decisions are untimed. Please report accessibility barriers with the relevant screen and assistive technology; do not send personal learner information.
