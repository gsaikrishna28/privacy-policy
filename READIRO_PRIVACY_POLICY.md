# Privacy Policy for Readiro

Effective date: October 25, 2026

Readiro is a life-skills learning app with interactive stories. This policy explains what the app stores, what it sends to other services, and how to contact us. Readiro does not require an account or a date of birth.

## Information stored on your device

Readiro stores your selected age range and learning interests, onboarding status, story choices and progress, completed stories, and any lesson takeaways you choose to save. A saved takeaway is curated story content, not a personal journal entry. You can also mark a suggested activity as tried. The app may keep local access to a story that was available to you before an access-policy change. A small local setting helps limit how often the app asks eligible adult-path learners for an App Store rating.

This information is used to personalize your path, resume stories, show progress, and remember saved ideas. Readiro does not collect answers to reflection prompts. Learning information remains in the app's local storage and is not sent to a developer-operated server. The app has no account system, advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs, public profiles, community messaging, or AI chat.

## Purchases

If you choose the one-time Lifetime Unlock, Apple's StoreKit handles the purchase and restoration. Readiro checks Apple's verified entitlement information to unlock eligible content. We do not receive your payment-card details through the app. Apple handles transaction and Apple Account information under its own policies. Loading a price, purchasing, or restoring may require a network connection.

## Backups, retention, and deletion

Learning information is retained on your device until you remove it or delete the app. You can remove individual saved takeaways in My Journey. Deleting Readiro removes its local learning data from that device; a device backup may still contain a copy until you manage or delete the backup through your device or backup provider. Readiro does not provide developer-hosted progress sync. Restoring a purchase restores eligible access, not deleted learning progress. Apple manages purchase records separately.

## Children and families

Readiro offers paths for ages 8–12 and 13–17. It asks for an age range rather than a birthday and does not ask children to create an account or public profile. Optional purchase information on younger paths is directed to a parent or guardian. A parent or guardian should handle purchases and support requests for a younger learner. The app's adult check is a prompt, not identity verification.

## Support and external policy page

If you email us for support, we receive the address and message you choose to send. We use that information to respond to your request. Please avoid sending a child's personal information, private reflections, payment details, or other sensitive information. You can contact us to ask about or request deletion of your support correspondence; we will handle requests under applicable requirements.

This policy is hosted on GitHub. If you open the public policy page from Readiro or an App Store listing, GitHub may process information about your visit under [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). Opening the page is optional; it is separate from using Readiro's learning features.
